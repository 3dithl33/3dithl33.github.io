Before my birthday that year, I lost my father, Nini, Niki, and maybe more.<br>
I believe my father drowned himself in his lotus pond to enjoy the eternal summer. And according to my mother, she sent my dogs, Niki and Nini, to somewhere better. As a seven-year-old, I felt extremely calm when everything happened; Later in life, I added a footnote to this situation: how can someone even feel anything if they learned the word “obituary” before they could even write the character for “love” on their own? At the time, I had a very chaotic concept of “being dead”. Most of the time, the people around me saw it as a drastically negative thing, but, as my mom told me, puppies and Dad had gone somewhere better. Sometimes I even thought I was being punished for “being alive”, and all I wanted was to go there and meet my dogs again.<br>
The year after, my mother promised to buy me Nintendogs if I went to school again and "acted normal." Nintendogs is a pet dog simulation game by Nintendo that debuted on the Nintendo DS. It is one of my favorite games of all time, and in this article I would like to analyze the reasons behind it.<br>
## The gamer engagement of being "reinforced" by Nintendogs
When you adopt your puppy in Nintendogs, there's a tutorial to be completed to help you familiarize the environment and rules of the game. One of the essential steps is using the microphone on your console to keep calling your puppy's name until it familiarizes it. Every time the puppy recognizes your voice and their name, a lightbulb sign appears on its head.<br>
I was thrilled when my dog responded to me whenever I called its name. It is not merely a joyful gaming experience, but the function fulfilled my emotions that were neglected by reality. I always felt I lost my voice when I was a kid because no matter how hard I tried to communicate with others, it never worked. I cried a lot, used bows to scrub my violin on a daily basis, and even screamed when I got angry. But all of these noises still made me feel like nobody could hear me.Bats' echolocation helps them distinguish their position, but as a kid, I couldn't sense my existence anymore because I could never "locate" myself when I talked.<br>
And in Nintendogs, the microphone substitutes my vocal cords to make sound so the dogs can hear me. In addition, more functions replace my human organs in the game. For example, when you pet your dog or take it out, you will control its hand through the touchable screen on your console. The incorporated and embodied gaming engagement satisfied me the most as a kid due to my failure to express myself in reality. I see the substitution as a cyborg-like phenomenon; taking Merleau-Ponty's theory from Phenomenology of Perception into account, my body and my perception are not in the relationship of agent and agency but a mutual one that is constantly integrating, even unconsciously. And now, my body is embodied by games, so my perception is also altered. The Nintendogs player version of me is a modified individual who will grow along with my progress in the game. Moreover, the hybridity of taking components to replace my missing parts is not merely about the material effect but also the ongoing progress of merging the game experience into myself.<br>
## The relationships with digital pets in Nintendogs
As a kid, I was very good at daydreaming. I would hide in the toilet in school, daydreaming and avoiding the bully. There are so many things I was too young to understand at home, so I also daydream about going through them. It is a very ordinary description of my childhood because every kid is good at daydreaming. But I also figure it is one of the biggest reasons I love Nintendogs. My desire to control my daily life is heavily reflected in my gaming behavior. I was trying so hard to train my dogs to win the competitions so we could earn money to buy pet supplies. Purchasing food and toys for your dog is the simplest way to fulfill its needs, and it was the reason I thrived in winning the competition. The mechanism in Nintendogs obeys capitalism, equating emotional needs and affection with commodities. And whoever gets the money has the dominant power, meaning my dog depends on my control and ability to be happy.<br>
I brought my expectations that I failed to fulfill in reality to the game and felt obligated to meet my dog's needs. I want it to be satisfied because its happiness is the most evident proof of my ability to control the world. The digital representation of "ownership" in Nintendogs allows me to create another identity that builds the world based on everything I want the most: affection, caring, and happiness, even though I was unaware that all the elements are based on inequality and capital power.<br>
Nintendogs, just like all the plushies in my childhood room, play an unignorable role in shaping my mind. The game is undoubtedly amazing but thinking about my motives and the situation I was in. I'm not certain about their influence on me, is it reinforcing me with the components I need, or stuffing me with cotton, just like how they make a plushie?<br>
